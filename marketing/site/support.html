Billing questions

Charges for Checkout Capture are made by Shopify and appear on your Shopify bill. The app's Home screen shows the checkouts counted in your current billing cycle and your plan. If you believe a charge is wrong, email us with your store's domain and the billing cycle, and we'll look into it with you.

Data and privacy requests

Merchants can export their leads at any time from the app. Shoppers who want to see or erase their data should contact the store they shopped at; the store sends the request to us through Shopify, and we handle it as described in the privacy policy. You can also write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
