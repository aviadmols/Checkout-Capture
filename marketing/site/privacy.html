2. Our role

For data about the merchants who install the app, we are the controller. For data about shoppers in a merchant's store, the merchant is the controller and we process that data on the merchant's behalf and on their instructions — the settings they choose in the app. Merchants are responsible for telling their shoppers about this processing in their own privacy notice and for having a valid basis for it, including marketing consent where the law requires it.

3. Data about merchants

When a store installs the app, we receive and store:

  • The store's name, .myshopify.com domain and storefront domain
  • Access tokens issued by Shopify for the permissions the merchant approved (stored encrypted)
  • The app settings the merchant chooses, including an optional webhook URL
  • Billing status of the app subscription, as reported by Shopify (we never see payment card details)
  • Usage records: the checkouts the app created, used to calculate the plan

4. Data about shoppers

Depending on the merchant's settings, the app collects, when a shopper provides it or arrives with it:

  • Contact details: email address, and if the merchant shows those fields, name, phone number and birthday
  • Consent choices: whether the shopper agreed to email or SMS marketing
  • How they were recognised: the popup, a link containing their email, or a form on the store
  • Cart details: cart token, products, quantities, prices, total and currency
  • Technical data: the page address, browser language, IP address and browser user agent
  • Order details: for orders placed by shoppers the app captured — order number, email, total and currency, received from Shopify

The app does not collect payment information, passwords or any data from Shopify's checkout pages.

6. How data is used

  • To attach the shopper's email to their cart, so Shopify's checkout opens with it filled in
  • To show the merchant their leads, abandoned carts, checkouts created and orders received
  • To detect abandoned carts and hand them to the tools the merchant chose (see Sharing)
  • To calculate usage for billing
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems

We do not sell personal data, use it for our own marketing, or use shoppers' data to send them messages. We do not use it to train machine-learning models.

7. Sharing

Shopper data is shared only as needed to provide the app, and only as the merchant configures it:

  • Shopify: the email (and name and phone, if collected) is written to the shopper's cart; abandoned carts can be sent to Shopify Flow, which may create or update the shopper as a customer in the merchant's store, subscribed to email marketing only if the shopper opted in.
  • The merchant's webhook: if the merchant sets one, abandoned carts are sent to that address. The merchant is responsible for the service at that address.
  • Klaviyo or Omnisend: if the merchant turns this on and runs those tools, the shopper's email is passed to them in the browser so they can recognise the shopper.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and the integrity of the service.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Shopper data is kept while the merchant uses the app, so the merchant can see and export it.
  • When a store uninstalls the app, its access ends immediately. Shopify sends a shop/redact request 48 hours later, and we then delete all of that store's data — settings, leads, carts, usage and order records.
  • When a customer's data must be erased, Shopify sends a customers/redact request and we delete that shopper's leads, carts, usage and order records for that store.
  • When a customer asks for their data, Shopify sends a customers/data_request and we provide the data we hold about them to the merchant within 30 days.
  • Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA or Israel's Privacy Protection Law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Shoppers: please contact the store you shopped at first — it controls your data and can send the request to us. Merchants: contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Shopify access tokens are encrypted at rest. Requests from the storefront are checked against the store's domains and rate-limited, Shopify webhooks are verified by signature, and the webhooks we send are signed with a per-store secret. Access to production systems is limited to the people who operate the app.

12. International transfers

The app's servers are in the United States. Where data is transferred from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

13. Children

The app is for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about children under 16.
