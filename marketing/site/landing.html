Recognise shoppers

Your campaigns already know who they are.

Add ?email= to the links in your emails and texts, and shoppers who click through are recognised without a form. Emails typed into your store's own newsletter or contact forms can count too.

  • Works with the merge tags of any email or SMS tool
  • The email is removed from the address bar right away
  • Store-form pickup is off until you turn it on

Abandoned carts

Abandoned carts, handed to the tools you already use.

When a recognised shopper leaves products in the cart and goes quiet, the cart is marked abandoned — with a link that rebuilds it and opens checkout with their email filled in. Your own tools send the message. We never email your shoppers.

  • A “Cart abandoned” trigger in Shopify Flow
  • A signed webhook for n8n, Make, Zapier or your own system
  • Klaviyo and Omnisend recognise the shopper, so their flows can run
  • You choose the idle time
