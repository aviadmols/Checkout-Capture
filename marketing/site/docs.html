Set up Checkout Capture

Everything the app does, in the order you'll set it up. Most stores are live in five minutes.

1. Install 2. Turn on the app embed 3. Choose how it works 4. Design the popup 5. Recognise shoppers 6. Abandoned carts 7. Webhook reference 8. Shopify Flow 9. Billing and usage 10. Data and uninstalling Troubleshooting

1. Install

Install Checkout Capture from the Shopify App Store and approve the permissions. The app opens inside your Shopify admin on its Home screen, with a short setup checklist. There's nothing to configure on our side and no code to paste.

The app asks for these permissions, and uses them only for what's listed:

PermissionWhy
Read ordersTo count the orders placed by shoppers the app captured, and show their revenue.
Read and write customersOnly for the Shopify Flow trigger: to find the shopper by email, or add them as a customer (subscribed to email marketing only if they opted in).
Storefront: read products and checkouts, write checkoutsTo attach the shopper's email to their cart, so checkout opens with it filled in.

2. Turn on the app embed

  1. On the app's Home screen, click Open theme editor. Or go to Online Store → Themes → Customize → App embeds.
  2. Switch Checkout Capture on and click Save.
  3. Back on Home, the first checklist step shows Live once your storefront has loaded the app.

The app embed works with every Online Store 2.0 theme. It doesn't run inside the theme editor, so editing your theme is never interrupted by the popup.

3. Choose how it works

In Settings → How it works, pick one mode:

ModeWhat shoppers see
Popup formShoppers we don't know yet get a short form when they click Checkout. Shoppers we already know go straight through.
Email from link onlyNo form at all. Shoppers who arrive from a link with their email (for example ?email= in a campaign) are recognised; everyone else checks out untouched.
OffThe app steps aside completely.

In every mode, logged-in customers never see a form, express checkout buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay) are not affected, and if the app can't load, checkout works exactly as before.

4. Design the popup

  • Layout: Classic (a centred card, a bottom sheet on mobile) or Split (your brand image beside the form, above it on mobile). Upload the image in Content → Files and paste its link.
  • Button and panel colour: any hex colour.
  • Language and texts: every text and field label can be translated. Choose left-to-right or right-to-left. Fill in English and Fill in Hebrew set all texts at once.
  • Form fields: email (always on), full name, first name, last name, phone, birthday, email marketing consent and SMS marketing consent. Show, hide, require and reorder them. Consent boxes are never pre-ticked and can't be required.
  • Behaviour: optionally let shoppers continue without filling the form.

Click Preview popup to see it with your saved settings. Nothing is saved or sent in the preview.

5. Recognise shoppers

From your campaign links

Add the shopper's email to any link to your store, using your email or SMS tool's merge tag:

https://yourstore.com/?email={{ email }}

The parameter name (email) can be changed in Settings. The email is saved, removed from the address bar immediately, and attached to the cart when the shopper checks out.

From your store's own forms

Turn on Pick up emails typed into your store's own forms to recognise shoppers who type their email into a newsletter, contact or account form. Off by default.

Remembering the shopper

A recognised shopper is remembered in their browser (localStorage and a first-party cookie named cc_details, for 180 days), so they aren't asked again on their next visit.

6. Abandoned carts

Turn on Track abandoned carts of recognised shoppers and set the idle time (5 to 1,440 minutes, default 30). When a recognised shopper has products in the cart and stays idle that long without heading to checkout, the cart is marked abandoned. You'll find it under Abandoned carts with a recovery link that rebuilds the cart on any device and opens checkout with the email filled in.

Checkout Capture never emails shoppers. Abandoned carts are handed to your own tools: Shopify Flow, a webhook, or Klaviyo and Omnisend.

Klaviyo and Omnisend

Turn on Identify recognised shoppers to Klaviyo / Omnisend. If either tool runs on your store, it's told who the shopper is, so its own abandoned-cart flows can run for shoppers who came from a link or a store form. Klaviyo may skip tracking for visitors in the EU and UK who haven't given cookie consent.

Shopify's own abandoned checkouts

Shopify creates an abandoned checkout only when a shopper reaches the checkout page. Because the app attaches the email before checkout, shoppers who reach checkout and leave can already have their email on that abandoned checkout.

7. Webhook reference

Paste an https:// URL in Send abandoned carts to (webhook URL). Each abandoned cart is sent once as a POST with a JSON body. Use Send test webhook to send a sample (event test).

HeaderValue
X-Checkout-Capture-Eventcart.abandoned or test
X-Checkout-Capture-Signaturesha256= + HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body, keyed with your signing secret (shown in Settings)
{
  "event": "cart.abandoned",
  "id": 42,
  "shop": { "name": "Northwind Goods", "myshopify_domain": "northwind.myshopify.com", "storefront_domain": "northwind.com" },
  "customer": { "email": "dana@example.com", "first_name": "Dana", "last_name": "Cohen", "phone": null,
                "accepts_marketing": true, "accepts_sms": false },
  "source": "url",
  "cart": { "token": "c1-…", "currency": "USD", "total_price": 247, "total_price_cents": 24700, "item_count": 3,
            "items": [{ "variant_id": 4410, "product_title": "Linen Overshirt", "variant_title": "Sand / M", "quantity": 2, "price": 89 }] },
  "recovery_url": "https://northwind.com/cart/4410:2?checkout%5Bemail%5D=dana%40example.com",
  "last_activity_at": "2026-10-15T10:00:00+00:00",
  "abandoned_at": "2026-10-15T10:30:00+00:00"
}

Verify the signature before trusting a request (Node.js):

const crypto = require('crypto');
const expected = 'sha256=' + crypto.createHmac('sha256', SECRET).update(rawBody).digest('hex');
const valid = crypto.timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(expected), Buffer.from(req.headers['x-checkout-capture-signature']));

Check customer.accepts_marketing (or accepts_sms) before you send a marketing message. Each delivery is tried up to three times; failures are shown as Failed under Abandoned carts, where you can resend them.

8. Shopify Flow

Every abandoned cart also fires the Cart abandoned trigger in Shopify Flow, with a customer reference — so you can build a workflow with Shopify Email's Send marketing email, tag the customer, or call any other Flow action. The shopper is found by email, or added as a customer; they're subscribed to email marketing only if they ticked the consent box.

Flow variableContents
CustomerThe Shopify customer
Customer emailThe shopper's email
Recovery urlRebuilds the cart and opens checkout with the email filled in
Cart total, CurrencyThe cart's value
ProductsFor example “Linen Overshirt × 2, Canvas Tote × 1”
SourceHow the shopper was recognised: url, popup, form or customer

9. Billing and usage

The first 50 checkouts in every 30-day billing cycle are free. Approve the plan on the app's Home screen to keep the app working past 50; Shopify then bills you, in USD, only in cycles that pass 50 checkouts: $14 for 51 to 1,000 checkouts and $25 in total above 1,000. A cycle never costs more than $25.

A checkout is one cart the app attached an email to and sent to checkout, counted once per cycle. Logged-in customers aren't counted. Without an approved plan, the app pauses once the free 50 are used, and your checkout keeps working as usual.

The Home screen shows this cycle's checkouts, your plan, the orders placed by shoppers the app captured, and their revenue.

10. Data and uninstalling

Your leads are under Leads, with search, filters and CSV export. When you uninstall, the app stops immediately and its access to your store ends. Shopify asks us to erase your store's data 48 hours later, and we delete it then. Requests to view or erase a customer's data, sent through Shopify, are handled as described in the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

The popup doesn't appear

  • Check that the app embed is on and saved (Home shows Live).
  • Check that the mode is Popup form.
  • You may already be recognised: open the store in a private window to test as a new shopper.
  • If your theme's checkout button is unusual, add its CSS selector under Extra checkout selectors.

The app paused

You've used the free 50 checkouts this cycle. Approve the plan on Home to resume.

Still stuck?

Contact support with your store's myshopify domain and what you see.
